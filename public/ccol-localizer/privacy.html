Privacy policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Chinese Chess Out Loud Localizer is a private desktop helper operated by Travis Mallett for his YouTube channel. It uses YouTube API Services to prepare and publish subtitles, translated titles and descriptions. This policy covers the helper, rather than Lixiangqi's chess service.

Data we use

The helper accesses the authorized channel's name and ID, selected video metadata, caption tracks and subtitle text. It uses these to verify ownership, create translations and update selected videos. Google authorization tokens allow these requests; the helper does not collect Google passwords.

Data sharing

Subtitle and metadata text, including draft translations, is sent to OpenAI through the configured API or Codex service for proofreading, translation and review. Google authorization tokens and client secrets are not included in translation requests. Google receives API requests and content selected for publication. The operator does not sell this data or use it for advertising.

Provider handling is governed by the applicable service terms and account settings. See the Google Privacy Policy and OpenAI Privacy Policy. Use and transfer of Google API information will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and removal

Saved Google authorization is encrypted on the owner's Windows computer. The original client credential file is kept privately. Source files, translations, caches and backups are stored locally as ordinary files until the owner deletes them; the helper does not automatically clean them up.

The owner can remove local job files, backups and credentials, and revoke access through Google account permissions. Revoking access does not delete local copies or translations already published to YouTube; those can be edited or removed separately in YouTube Studio.

This website and contact

These informational pages contain no sign-in form, analytics scripts or tracking cookies of their own. Hosting infrastructure may process routine connection information to serve and protect the pages. The helper and its credentials are not hosted here.

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact Travis Mallett at dweeberkitty@gmail.com. Changes to these practices will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
