# Cannon puzzle-theme artwork

Original artwork generated with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool for LiXiangQi. The former cannon-category placeholders were not opened or used as design references.

## Visual direction

Painted malachite green, aged bronze and warm ivory establish a third category alongside the cinnabar chariots and indigo horses. The same dark contours, modeled material surfaces and pale edge highlights preserve the family resemblance. The eight motifs use different silhouettes and meaningful objects rather than repeated cannon badges or board diagrams.

Palette: malachite `#3f7752`, pale fern-jade `#a9c58a`, forest ink `#263b2d`, aged bronze `#b09459`, warm ivory `#e9dfbd`. These specify the direction of the generated paintings rather than flat-color pixel values.

## Meaning and interpretation

| Method   | Xiangqi content                                                                                  | Icon interpretation                                                                   |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 重炮杀   | Two cannons cooperate on one line; the nearer one provides the farther one's screen.             | Two separately readable bronze barrels in a staggered tandem silhouette.              |
| 天地炮   | The central-file and back-rank cannons constrain different palace defenses.                      | A high cannon borne by a pale cloud and a low cannon on green rock: heaven and earth. |
| 闷宫     | The general's own defenders prevent escape from a cannon check.                                  | Tightly closed palace doors compressed between their posts and lintel.                |
| 铁门栓   | A cannon pins central defenders while a protected chariot or soldier seals the general's escape. | A heavy iron sliding bar locks two fragments of gate timber together.                 |
| 马后炮   | A horse controls escape and provides the cannon's screen.                                        | An alert horse in front of a separate supporting cannon behind it.                    |
| 夹车炮   | A chariot alternates attacks with the cannons surrounding it.                                    | A central chariot wheel flanked by two supporting cannon barrels.                     |
| 平顶冠   | The finishing attack is likened to placing a cap above the general.                              | A flat-topped Chinese ceremonial headdress with a short curtain of ivory beads.       |
| 双杯献酒 | The first cannon offering breaks the defense; the next completes the attack.                     | Two shallow oval ear cups containing amber wine, staggered as successive offerings.   |

These are original illustrative metaphors, not depictions of the exact move sequence or claims that each is an established traditional emblem. The rendered doors are real-world architectural objects, not the nine-point palace from the board.

The distinction between 闷宫 and 铁门栓 is deliberate: enclosure versus fastening. The former shows an entire closed doorway; the latter concentrates on the physical locking mechanism.

For 双杯献酒, the Chinese instructional explanation found was the metaphor of two successive cups overcoming someone whom one cup did not intoxicate. No reliable source found in this research established the suggested execution-by-poison origin, so the icon does not assert it or add poison symbols. Ear cups supply the Chinese vessel shape; their green finish is an art-direction choice, not an archaeological reconstruction.

For 平顶冠, the flat board and hanging bead strands come from Chinese ceremonial/operatic headdress forms. This icon is simplified for small sizes; it is not a precise reconstruction of court rank, bead count or historical regalia. It uses no European crown points or heraldry.

The early Chinese bronze bowl-mouth cannon is the reference for the barrel language. Game-scale proportions and the green material treatment are stylized; the artwork is not a weapons reconstruction. There are no European wheeled gun carriages.

## Research sources

- [Chinese instructional text, 图说少儿象棋入门](https://media.metaisach.com/2025/02/do-thuyet-thieu-nhi-tuong-ky-nhap-mon-cc3813f9.pdf): the paired-wine metaphor and the division of roles in 天地炮.
- [GameTea: 平顶冠](https://www.gametea.com/news/201205/3006.html): describes the finish as placing a cap above the opposing general.
- [Xiangqi Museum: 炮碾丹沙](https://www.xqmuseum.cn/articledetail/221.html): discusses 平顶冠 alongside related coordinated cannon attacks.
- [Chinese Intangible Cultural Heritage Museum: theatrical headdresses](https://www.ihchina.cn/Article/Index/detail?id=22011): identifies the flat crown board and hanging bead strands of 平顶冠/平天冠.
- [Palace Museum: ear cups](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/tinware/232659.html): explains the oval bowl, flat base and side ears, and their use for wine and food.
- [National Museum of China: bronze cannon](https://www.chnmuseum.cn/zp/zpml/kgfjp/202111/t20211116_252348.shtml): the early bowl-mouth bronze cannon and mounting context.
- The canonical lesson descriptions in `translation/source/puzzleTheme.xml` and examples in `PuzzleThemeLesson.scala` were checked for the site's exact eight methods.

These sources supplied cultural and tactical context. No external artwork was copied or passed to the generator. The newly generated chariot-wheel icon supplied rendering-style reference for the initial cannon and latch images. The new double-cannon image supplied the cannon family's material and palette reference for the remaining six.

## Delivery

Eight canonical 256 × 256 transparent WebP assets in `public/images/puzzle-themes/`, quality 90. Generated alpha and painted colors are preserved during proportional downsampling, with artwork centered in a 236 × 236 area. Near-invisible alpha noise is ignored only when determining crop bounds. No separate light/dark artwork, PNG duplicates or SVG fallbacks are shipped.

The existing circular mounts and card theme colors remain unchanged. `PuzzleTheme.iconFile` supplies filenames to the themes page and puzzle sidebar. The two dedicated placeholder SVGs are removed; the shared `mix.svg` remains for other methods that still use it. The deployment validator already enumerates current source assets, so it requires no code change.
